This plays the audio with the screen drawn live, so you can see whether each line lights up when it is sung. There are two ways to line them up. Steady tempo: type the beats a minute and the second the first line starts; every line is then placed by counting beats. Tapping: play the audio and press the big button (or the space bar) at the moment each line starts. Tapped lines show a green time, and lines you did not tap are spaced between the tapped ones. Click any line to jump there and tap again from that line. Nothing here changes the audio.
