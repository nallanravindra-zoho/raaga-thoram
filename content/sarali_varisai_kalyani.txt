SARALI VARISAI (SARALI SWARAMULU) FOR KALYANI RAGAM
============================================================

Raga: Kalyani (Mechakalyani, the 65th melakarta)
Scale going up:   S R2 G3 M2 P D2 N3 Ś
Scale coming down: Ś N3 D2 P M2 G3 R2 S
In Kalyani the Ma is the sharp one (prati madhyamam). It is written as plain M below,
exactly as on your sheet, which was for Mayamalavagowla / Shankarabharanam.
Only the Ma changes when these exercises are sung in Kalyani.

HOW TO READ
  S R G M P D N   the seven notes;   Ś = the high Sa
  ,               hold the note before it for one more beat
  |               a break between parts of the tala
  ||              the end of one cycle (avartanam) of 8 beats
Tala: Adi talam = laghu (4 beats) + drutam (2) + drutam (2) = 8 beats.
Each line below is 16 beats = 2 cycles.

SPEEDS (the beat tempo stays the same)
  Speed 1: 1 note per beat
  Speed 2: 2 notes per beat (the same notes twice as fast)
  Speed 3: 4 notes per beat
  The comma means a repeat of previous known note
