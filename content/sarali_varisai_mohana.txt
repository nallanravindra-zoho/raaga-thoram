SARALI VARISAI FOR MOHANA RAGAM (derived from the Kalyani exercises)
============================================================

Raga: Mohana
Scale going up:    S R2 G3 P D2 Ś
Scale coming down: Ś D2 P G3 R2 S
Mohana has no M, N. Wherever an exercise has one of those notes the singer holds the note before it
and sings "aa" (shown as _), so the rhythm, the 8-beat cycles and the speeds stay the same.
These exercises are DERIVED, not copied from a teacher's sheet: please have them checked.

HOW TO READ
  S R G P D   the notes;   Ś = the high Sa
  ,   hold the note before it for one more beat
  _   a note this raga does not have: it is sung as a long "aa" (the screen shows aa, not a note name)
  |   a break between parts of the tala
  ||  the end of one cycle (avartanam) of 8 beats
Tala: Adi talam = laghu (4 beats) + drutam (2) + drutam (2) = 8 beats.
Each line is 16 beats = 2 cycles.
Speeds: 1 note per beat, 2 per beat, 4 per beat (the beat tempo stays the same).
